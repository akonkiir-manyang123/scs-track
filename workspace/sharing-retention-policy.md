# Sharing and retention policy -- SCS3.2

**Status:** Practice-business draft. The owner must confirm all settings and retention periods before this is used as an actual business policy.

**AI-assisted, directed by me.** I reviewed and directed this draft. I must verify every setting against the actual Admin console and the owner's instructions.

## Where files live

Business working files belong in the approved shared drive, not in an employee's personal My Drive. The owner should identify the approved shared-drive name and its managers. Access should be given to people who need each folder for their work.

## Sharing outside the business

External sharing should be restricted unless the owner approves a specific business need. Staff should not make customer or business files public or share them using unrestricted links. Check the sharing settings in the Admin console and test with an account outside the business before approving this policy.

## Who can sign in

Only approved staff accounts may sign in. Each person should use their own account. Require 2-step verification for staff where the workspace supports it. Check enrolment in the users list and investigate accounts that have not enrolled.

## What we keep, how long, and where

Keep only records needed for business operations, legal duties, and customer service. Store approved records in the shared drive. The owner must confirm the retention period for each record type and any legal requirements before a number of months or years is entered here. Do not invent a retention period.

## Monthly export

The designated administrator should export the users list and relevant audit records monthly, store them in the restricted evidence or records location, and record the export date and person responsible. Confirm the actual export location and access permissions with the owner.

## Who holds admin

The owner should name the primary administrator and an approved backup administrator. Admin access must be limited to people who need it. Use the least powerful role that allows each person to do their job. Record role changes in the admin change log.

## When someone leaves

When a staff member leaves, suspend their account first. Confirm that they can no longer sign in. Transfer business files to the approved successor or owner, review shared-drive access, and check that remaining staff still have the access they need. Do not delete the account until the owner has approved the retention and transfer checks.
